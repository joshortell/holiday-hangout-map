Quick Host Cheat Sheet

  1. Open & Share: Open the Live App and share that browser tab in Google Meet.
  2. Add an Attendee: Click + Add Attendee (or press A) — or click any US State, D.C. & US Territories, or International directly on the map.
  3. Enter Pets: Tap + for Dogs, Cats, Birds, Fish, or Other Pets, then click Add & Show Trivia.
  4. Trivia Time: Read the question aloud, click Reveal Answer (or press Space), then click Continue Slideshow.
  5. Auto-Rotating Views: The Map, Pets, and Leaderboard rotate every 10 seconds automatically. Click any tab (or press 1, 2, 3) or Pause anytime.
  6. Fix Typos: Click ☰ Log & Tools to Undo Last, delete a specific entry, or change the slideshow speed.
Practice & Live Sync: Feel free to practice right now! All open hosts stay synced automatically in real time. All practice data & trivia will automatically clear at 12:50 PM ET on Thanksgiving Day (10 minutes before the 1:00 PM ET start). On Christmas Day at 12:50 PM ET, counts clear automatically while keeping Thanksgiving's trivia marked used so questions won't repeat.
